Guidelines for simulated wallet balances and guided partner tour reservations

1. Tourez Wallet Demo Simulator

Because the current Tourez Wallet is a free client-side planning simulator, balances represent demo units rather than legal tender. No fees are ever billed to your credit card. You can reset your demo balance to NPR 0 at any time using the "Reset demo wallet" button.

2. Partner Guided Treks & Bookings

When booking guided trekking packages, transport, or hotel services through accredited Tourez local partners, the following standard Nepal Tourism Board-compliant refund timetable applies:

Cancellation Window Refund Eligibility Notes
7+ Days Prior to Departure 100% Full Refund Subject to 3% bank/wire processing fee.
3 to 7 Days Prior to Departure 50% Refund Partial retention for pre-booked guide logistics.
Under 48 Hours or No-Show Non-Refundable Permits (TIMS/ACAP) & staff wages are non-recoverable.

3. Monsoon & Mountain Weather Force Majeure

In cases of domestic mountain flight cancellations (e.g., Kathmandu–Lukla or Pokhara–Jomsom flights) due to fog, monsoons, or extreme mountain weather, travelers are offered immediate rescheduling or credit vouchers for overland jeep transportation.

4. Filing a Refund or Dispute

To request a refund review for a partner booking, please email refunds@tourez.com.np with your booking reference ID within 14 calendar days of the scheduled departure.
